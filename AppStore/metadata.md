## Description
Boundless Meditation is a calm, open-ended timer for meditation. There's no countdown and no finish line: start a session and the clock simply counts up for as long as you sit.

Every few minutes a gentle mark lets you know how long you've been sitting without opening your eyes.

GENTLE MARKS
• A mark every 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes (5 by default).
• Every Nth mark is a major mark (every 10 minutes by default), so you can feel the difference between 5 and 10 without counting.

APPLE WATCH
• A soft tap on your wrist at every mark, with a different tap for major marks.
• Keeps tapping with your wrist down and the screen off.
• Standard mode needs no permissions. Long session mode runs as a Mind & Body workout for sits longer than an hour, and only saves to Health if you turn that on.
• The screen always tells you whether wrist-down taps are active.

IPHONE
• A synthesized singing-bowl chime at each mark, with a deeper bowl for major marks.
• Keeps chiming with your phone locked, and mixes politely with music.
• Works on its own, no watch required.

TOGETHER
• Settings sync between iPhone and Apple Watch, and start, pause and end are mirrored while both apps are open.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing leaves your devices.
